Start Line · Ping & Bias

For Hut starts, the Hut end is fixed and only the Pin End is pinged. For committee starts, ping the Committee Boat and Pin End. The line is compared with the active wind direction.

The app projects this device position forward to the estimated bow using the First 31.7 LOA of 9.61 m. Choose the location where the phone/tablet is actually being held when you ping.
